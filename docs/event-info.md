## Practical stuff
- **JetBrains:** free OpenAI API keys, one per team. Ask at their booth or on Discord. Don't push keys to GitHub (they get blocked). Their coding agent is free until end of year. JetBrains IDEs are free while you're a student.
- **JetBrains judging doc:** QR code at their booth, also posted in Discord.
- **Discord** is the comms channel for sponsors.
- Sponsors have booths/stalls; Bank of Scotland is there all weekend.

## Judging details worth knowing
- **Instalily:** part automatic, part subjective.
  - Automatic: an evaluator harness (team kit) plugs into your solution and runs simple hidden tests, so the agent must actually fix the issues.
  - "Adaptive user": the product must adapt to different users.
  - Live demo: their engineers will use your product ("win over real people").
  - You must pick a persona and design for it.
- **Viridien:**
  - Dataset: 50 long documents.
  - Must generalise to unseen data. Test data is released at the end for you to demo on.
  - Judged on accuracy and efficiency.
  - Suggested approach: segmentation + clustering, but classic CV also fine.
  - Reports can be in any language (English, German, Spanish...).
- **Wordsmith tiebreaker:** token use, model use, compute efficiency.
- **HRT:** data can be anything; it doesn't have to be financial.

## Tips
- Several tracks can be combined (Wordsmith explicitly encourages overlap). MetaView's prize accepts any project.
- Most AI-friendly tracks: JetBrains (needs AI-assisted coding), Instalily (agentic IDE), Wordsmith (browser agents). A single "agentic dev tool" project could plausibly hit JetBrains + Instalily + MetaView.
- Easiest cash: JetBrains €350/person, MetaView $500/person, Bank of Scotland £150/person.
